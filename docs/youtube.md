**Description:**

Precedent is an Atlassian Forge app that remembers what your team decided — in Confluence decision pages, decision items and Jira "Decision:" comments — and checks new work against it wherever the work happens:

• Jira: a "Past decisions" panel that says "1 conflict" before anyone opens it, with the decision, why it was made, and who owns it (via Teamwork Graph)
• Confluence: a byline on RFCs and design docs that flags conflicts while the author is still writing
• Rovo: the Precedent agent explains conflicts, cites decisions and records new ones
• MCP: coding agents (here, Claude) call Precedent before writing code
• Decision register + "Work at risk": every open work item that contradicts a decision

No LLM inside the app (fast, free, explainable checks), permission-filtered for every viewer (including over MCP), no personal data stored, Runs on Atlassian.
